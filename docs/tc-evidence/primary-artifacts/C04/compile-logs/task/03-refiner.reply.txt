What changed, and why it closes the hole: the old spec sampled `pop` at `-1`/`-2` only, so the acceptance boundary was never pinned. The new `case_accepted_index_domain_is_exactly_len_bounded` asserts the *predicate* — accept iff `-n <= i < n`, with `__getitem__` and `pop` returning identical verdicts — swept over every index in `[-2n-2, 2n+2)` on six fixtures including `n == 1` and `n == 2`, where every off-by-one variant (`<=`, `abs() >=`, clamp) collapses onto a tested index. `case_pop_matches_getitem_for_every_valid_index` adds the value-level equivalence, and `case_pop_min_negative_still_works` adopts the counter-probe verbatim in spirit. R9's `index()` round-trip guards collateral damage if the fix moves normalization into the shared `_get_real_index` helper.
